SCHOOL PROFILE

A school rooted in education and community

Government Boys High School Danyore is a government secondary-level boys' school located in Danyore, District Gilgit, Gilgit-Baltistan.

ABOUT THE SCHOOL

Education with purpose

The school represents an important part of the educational landscape of Danyore. Its identity is documented through government education records and examination systems.

This website brings together the school's institutional information, historical leadership record and publicly documented identifiers in one place.

A documented institutional journey

Key milestones reflected in available institutional and government records.

Institutional Record

Government Education Institution

Government Boys High School Danyore is documented in Gilgit-Baltistan education records under EMIS code 13016.

Examination Identity

FBISE Institution Code 66841

The school is listed by the Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education as a secondary-level institution.

Examination Centre

Danyore Male Examination Centre

Government records also identify the Danyore Male Examination Centre with centre code 6841.
